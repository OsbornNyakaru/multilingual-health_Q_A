# Competition Overview

**Zindi — Multilingual Health Question Answering in Low-Resource African Languages.**
Answer maternal, sexual and reproductive health questions in Luganda, Kiswahili, Akan/Twi, Amharic and English (the English rows are the majority, see [[Data Schema]]).

- Team: Chiromo Forge (lead: the repo owner).
- Window recorded in the README: 2026-04-24 to 2026-07-14. `train.py` says due 2026-06-21. The competition is now over; we are re-running it as practice on [[Molab Platform]].
- Data drop: 2026-04-30 (Train / Val / Test / SampleSubmission). Starter notebook and two host PDFs (HASH presentation, Zindi webinar) were added 2026-05-20/21.
- Challenge URL: https://zindi.africa/competitions/multilingual-health-question-answering-in-low-resource-african-languages-challenge
- Submission budget in the original rules: 50 total, 5 per day. Final two picked manually (see `scripts/select_final.py`).
- Zindi audits top-10 code, hence the reproducibility rules in [[Repo Layout]] (pinned deps, five-place seeding).
